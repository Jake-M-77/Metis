# Witness Section

A comprehensive **witness section** within an investigation report should provide a clear, objective, and auditable account of what each individual observed or experienced.

## Core Structure of a Witness Section

### 1. Witness Identification & Profile

Establishes who the witness is and their relationship to the investigation.

- **Full Legal Name:** The witness's full name, including any known aliases where relevant.
- **Contact Information:** Current address, phone number, and email address where appropriate.
- **Employment Details:** Job title, employer, or professional role where relevant.
- **Relationship to the Incident:** The witness's connection to the incident or the parties involved.

### 2. Interview Logistics

Records the circumstances under which the witness was interviewed.

- **Interview Date & Time:** The date and time the interview took place.
- **Interview Location:** The specific location where the interview was conducted.
- **Participants:** Names and roles of everyone present, such as investigators, legal representatives, or union representatives.
- **Recording Method:** The method used to document the interview, such as audio recording, video recording, written notes, or a formal written statement.

### 3. Narrative Account

Records the witness's firsthand observations and experiences.

- **Chronological Narrative:** A factual, chronological account of what the witness observed or experienced.
- **Observations:** What the witness saw, heard, or otherwise directly perceived.
- **Actions:** What the witness said or did during or immediately following the incident.
- **Knowledge Boundaries:** Clearly distinguish between what the witness personally observed and information they received from other sources.

### 4. Credibility & Consistency Assessment

Provides objective information about the reliability and evidential value of the witness's account.

- **Statement Consistency:** Whether the witness's account remains consistent across interviews or statements.
- **Corroboration:** Any physical, digital, or testimonial evidence that supports the witness's account.
- **Opportunity to Observe:** Factors affecting the witness's ability to observe the incident, such as line of sight, lighting, distance, duration, or obstructions.
- **Potential Limitations:** Any factors that may affect the completeness or accuracy of the witness's recollection.

### 5. Exhibits & Supporting Evidence

Documents evidence provided or identified by the witness.

- **Physical Evidence:** Any physical items provided or identified by the witness.
- **Digital Evidence:** Text messages, photographs, videos, emails, or other digital material.
- **Documents:** Handwritten notes, records, correspondence, or other relevant documentation.
- **Evidence References:** References to exhibits, evidence records, or other investigation materials associated with the witness.

### 6. Review & Sign-Off

Records the witness's acknowledgement of their statement.

- **Statement Review:** Confirmation that the witness was given an opportunity to review the written statement.
- **Corrections & Amendments:** Any corrections, additions, or amendments made by the witness.
- **Witness Acknowledgement:** Confirmation that the statement accurately reflects the witness's account.
- **Signature & Date:** The witness's signature and the date of acknowledgement where applicable.
- **Investigator Confirmation:** Identification and acknowledgement of the investigator responsible for recording the statement.
